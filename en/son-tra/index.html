    Son Tra Peninsula

    A forested peninsula by the sea, known for the giant Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda.

    At a glance

    Location

    About 10 km from central Da Nang

    Time needed

    2–3 hours

    Best time

    Morning or late afternoon

    Highlights

    • The roughly 67 m Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda looks out to sea
    • Known as the “green lung” of Da Nang
    • Home to the rare red-shanked douc langur

    About Son Tra Peninsula

    Son Tra is a lush peninsula jutting out east of Da Nang, with primary forest that contrasts with the city. Often called Da Nang’s “green lung”, it is a valuable area where rich nature survives. Mountains of roughly 700 m rise right from the sea, and from the top you can see the ocean and the city.

    The peninsula is known as home to the rare red-shanked douc langur. To protect wildlife, please never feed the animals or approach them closely.

    Linh Ung Pagoda and the Lady Buddha

    The peninsula’s main sight is the Lady Buddha statue at Linh Ung Pagoda (Bai But). About 67 m tall, it is among the tallest Lady Buddha statues in Vietnam, and it is said to watch over fishermen and ships. Its white, graceful figure is clearly visible from far away.

    The grounds have lovely gardens and viewing spaces facing the sea, a calm setting for a quiet visit and a stroll. Dress so your shoulders and knees are covered, and keep your voice low.

    Drives and views

    The road around the peninsula winds between sea and forest and is popular for drives and motorbike tours. Viewpoints along the way look out over Da Nang’s coastline, My Khe Beach and the city. Sunrise and dusk are especially picturesque.

    The mountain road has many bends and slopes, so drive carefully. On a tour you can travel in a car with a driver for peace of mind. In bad weather visibility drops, so put safety first.

    Suggested plan and tips

    A classic plan is to visit Linh Ung Pagoda in the morning, enjoy the viewpoints, then move on to My Khe Beach or the Marble Mountains. It fits easily into a half-day Da Nang city tour and is not tiring. Early mornings are cool and can be misty, so adjust to the weather.

    Good to know

    Son Tra is closer than you might expect, about 20 to 30 minutes by car from central Da Nang. It is easy to reach from the airport and city hotels, which makes it a good stop even on a short stay. On a clear day the sea and city are visible from near the top, a view that makes a lasting memory.

    Planning tips

    Bring comfortable shoes, sunscreen and drinks. If you meet wildlife, keep your distance and watch quietly. Take your rubbish with you and respect the environment.
