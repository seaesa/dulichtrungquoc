    My Khe Beach

    Da Nang’s signature beach, a long stretch of white sand.

    At a glance

    Location

    About 3 km from central Da Nang

    Time needed

    1–3 hours, longer if you relax

    Best time

    Morning walks or sunset; swimming season is roughly April–September

    Highlights

    • Gentle waves and a wide beach
    • Plenty of seafood restaurants and hotels nearby
    • Early mornings are lively with locals swimming and exercising

    About My Khe Beach

    My Khe is a long white-sand beach east of central Da Nang and one of the city’s best-known attractions. The shoreline runs for several kilometres, the water is shallow and the waves are fairly gentle on many days, so it suits swimming and walks. It has also been featured by overseas media as one of the world’s attractive beaches.

    Hotels, resorts, coffee shops and seafood restaurants line the beach, and it is loved by locals as well as visitors. It is close to the city, so it is easy to drop in whenever you like.

    Things to do

    Early morning is lively with locals swimming, doing exercises and jogging. Walking the sand at sunrise is a very Da Nang experience. By day you can rent a parasol and beach chair and relax, and in the evening enjoy the sun going down on the horizon.

    Some spots offer water sports, surfing and stand-up paddleboarding for active visitors. If you have small children, let them play in areas with lifeguards for peace of mind.

    Best season and cautions

    The best time for swimming is roughly April to September, the dry season, with more sunny days and clearer water. From about October to December there are more rainy and rough days, so check the weather before you go.

    On rough days, no-swimming flags are raised. Always follow them and do not swim against the warning. The sun is very strong, so bring sunscreen, a hat and plenty of water.

    Food and a suggested plan

    Seafood restaurants near the beach serve shrimp, squid, shellfish and seafood hotpots. Check prices and portions before you order. A classic day is a morning walk and coffee on the beach, a seafood lunch by the sea, and sunset in the evening.

    My Khe is close to the Marble Mountains, Linh Ung Pagoda and the Dragon Bridge, so it combines easily with a Da Nang city tour.

    Good to know

    My Khe is often within walking distance of hotels and easy to reach by taxi or Grab. Some places along the beach offer showers, toilets and changing rooms, handy for changing and rinsing off the sand. To avoid the strongest sun, rest in the shade or indoors between about 10 am and 3 pm.

    Planning tips

    Leave valuables at the hotel and take only what you need to the beach. Vendors and touts may approach you on the sand, and a clear “no, thank you” is enough. Check rental prices for chairs and parasols beforehand.
